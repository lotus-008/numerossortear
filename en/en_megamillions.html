How to Play Mega Millions

Mega Millions is one of America's two biggest lottery games, available across the U.S. Select 5 numbers from 1 to 70 (white balls) and 1 Mega Ball from 1 to 24 (gold ball). Each play costs $5, with a built-in Multiplier (2×–10×) included at no extra cost.

Drawings are held every Tuesday and Friday at 11:00 p.m. ET. The jackpot starts at $50 million.

What is the Mega Ball?

The Mega Ball is a gold ball drawn independently from a pool of 1 to 24. You need all 5 white balls plus the Mega Ball to win the jackpot. Matching just the Mega Ball alone wins $10.

Why use random numbers?

Every combination has the same odds. Our generator uses crypto.getRandomValues() — the cryptographic standard used in online banking — for true, unbiased randomness.

When are Mega Millions drawings?

Every Tuesday and Friday at 11:00 p.m. ET. Sales cut-off varies by state, typically 15–45 minutes before the drawing.

How does Mega Millions differ from Powerball?

Mega Millions: 5 from 1–70 + Mega Ball 1–24, $5/play, Tue & Fri. Powerball: 5 from 1–69 + Powerball 1–26, $2/play, Mon, Wed & Sat.
