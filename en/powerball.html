How to Play Powerball

Powerball is one of America's most popular lottery games, available in 45 states, Washington D.C., Puerto Rico, and the U.S. Virgin Islands. To play, select 5 numbers from 1 to 69 (white balls) and 1 Powerball number from 1 to 26 (red ball). Each play costs $2.

Drawings are held every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at 10:59 p.m. ET. The jackpot starts at $20 million and grows until someone wins.

What is the Powerball?

The Powerball is a separate red ball drawn from a pool of 1 to 26, independently from the 5 white balls. You need all 5 white balls plus the Powerball to win the jackpot. The Powerball can be the same number as one of your white balls.

Why use random numbers?

Every combination has exactly the same odds. Our generator uses crypto.getRandomValues() — the same cryptographic standard used in online banking — guaranteeing true, unbiased randomness.
